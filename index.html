Who we are

Where classrooms meetthe world’s campuses

UniBridge brings students, families, schools and leading universities into one thoughtful conversation. We bring a curated panel of universities into your school for a day, with guidance that makes big decisions feel clear, exciting and within reach.

  • Curated, never crowded: every conversation is intentional
  • Guidance that is honest, balanced and free of pressure
  • Universities and institutions from across the world

How it works

One school day,four simple steps

A calm, well-organised day that fits around your timetable and puts students first.

1

Plan with your school

We agree the date, the students and the format with your leadership team and counsellors.

2

Choose the universities

We curate a panel of universities matched to your students’ interests and routes.

3

The school day

Universities meet students in organised sessions, with space for parents where it suits.

4

Follow up

Students and families leave with clear next steps, and we stay on hand for questions.
